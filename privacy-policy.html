Privacy Policy for 57Living / n8n Automation

1. Information We Collect

Our n8n automation application connects various tools to streamline business workflows. We only access and process the data explicitly authorized by the user through Meta's OAuth authentication. We do not independently harvest, store, or sell personal user data.

Any data processed through our Meta application is used solely for the purpose of executing user-configured automated workflows (e.g., managing property rental inquiries or data syncing).

We do not retain user data on our servers beyond what is technically required to execute immediate automation tasks. All data transmissions are encrypted.
